A great virtual assistant can take hours of work off your plate every week. The difference between a VA who helps in week one and one who is still "getting set up" in week three is almost always onboarding.

Here is the checklist we use to get clients from first login to real output in 48 hours.

## Before day one: decide what to hand off

Spend 30 minutes listing the tasks you want to delegate. For each one, note:

- **How often** it happens (daily, weekly, ad hoc).
- **Which tools** it touches (inbox, calendar, CRM, spreadsheets).
- **What "done" looks like**, in one sentence.

Start with three to five repeatable tasks. Inbox triage, calendar management and CRM updates are good first candidates.

## Hours 0-4: access and security

Give access through accounts, never shared passwords:

1. Create a company email account for your VA.
2. Invite them to your tools with the **least privilege** they need.
3. Use a password manager to share any credentials that cannot be delegated.
4. Turn on two-factor authentication everywhere.

Every Akrostech engagement starts with an NDA, but good access hygiene protects you regardless of who you work with.

## Hours 4-12: record, don't write

Writing a perfect SOP takes days. Recording yourself doing the task takes minutes. For each task on your list:

- Record a short screen video while you do it once, talking through your decisions.
- Ask your VA to turn the recording into a written checklist. This doubles as a check that they understood it.

## Hours 12-36: do it together, then alone

For each task, run three passes:

1. **Watch.** Your VA watches you, or the recording.
2. **Shadow.** Your VA does the task while you review in real time.
3. **Solo.** Your VA does it alone and sends a summary.

Most tasks move from shadow to solo within a day.

## Hours 36-48: set the operating rhythm

Agree on how you will work together from here:

- **A daily summary** at the end of their shift: what was done, what is blocked, what is next.
- **One channel for urgent requests** and one for everything else.
- **A weekly 15-minute review** to add tasks and adjust priorities.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- **Handing off everything at once.** Start narrow, then expand.
- **Skipping the "done" definition.** Ambiguity is the most common cause of rework.
- **No time-zone overlap.** Your VA should work your hours. Ours work EST, CST, MST or PST.
