When founders compare an in-house hire with an offshore professional, they usually compare two salaries. That comparison is misleading, because salary is only the most visible line on the bill.

This guide walks through the costs that actually show up, so you can make the decision with the full picture.

## The costs that sit on top of salary

An onshore employee costs more than their pay. Before they do a day of work, you are also paying for:

- **Payroll taxes and benefits.** Employer taxes, health cover, retirement contributions and paid leave.
- **Recruiting.** Job ads, recruiter fees and the hours your team spends interviewing.
- **Equipment and space.** A laptop, software licences and, for many roles, a desk.
- **Ramp-up time.** Weeks of reduced output while the new hire learns your tools and processes.
- **Turnover risk.** If the hire leaves, you pay most of those costs again.

A dedicated offshore professional through a partner like Akrostech bundles most of that into one predictable monthly fee. Recruiting, payroll, equipment and replacement are handled for you.

## Where offshore talent saves money

For roles that can be done remotely, the savings come from three places:

1. **Lower cost of living** in the talent's home market, which means lower total compensation for the same skill level.
2. **No recruiting overhead.** Candidates come pre-vetted, so you skip most of the hiring funnel.
3. **Faster ramp-up.** Professionals who already know U.S. tools and workflows are productive sooner.

For roles like virtual assistance, our clients typically save **up to 70%** compared with a U.S.-based employee in the same role.

> The goal is not the cheapest possible hire. It is the best output per dollar, delivered reliably.

## What to watch out for

Offshore hiring goes wrong when quality and communication are treated as afterthoughts. Before you commit, check that your partner offers:

- **Time-zone alignment.** Your professional should work your business hours, not overlap for one hour a day.
- **Dedicated resources.** A person who works only for you, not a shared pool.
- **Quality checks.** Structured reporting and performance tracking, not just a timesheet.
- **Security and compliance.** NDAs, access controls and clear data-handling rules.

## A simple way to compare

Build a one-page comparison with two columns: the fully loaded annual cost of an onshore hire, and the annual fee for a dedicated offshore professional. Then add one more row that is easy to forget: **time to productivity**. Weeks saved are worth real money.

If the numbers favour offshore, start small. Bring on one professional for a well-defined set of tasks, measure the results for a month, then scale.
