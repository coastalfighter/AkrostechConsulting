# Surface: marketing site (all public pages)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: U.S. founders, ops/finance leaders, staffing agencies, CPA and law firms, weighed equally. Action: book the 30-minute discovery call (Calendly) or send an enquiry. Proof: published figures (100+ professionals, 98% accuracy, 48h onboarding, 24h shortlist, 5-stage vetting), U.S.-hours mechanism, labelled engagement blueprints. Constraint: every page hero is an interactive graphic with page-specific text, no two alike; stock photography wherever imagery is needed; black + lime #C9FF4A brand and the M-wave mark.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is Akrostech's own standards manual. Every page is a specification sheet on a strict twelve-column grid where black plates, whole lime fields and real photographs occupy measured modules, and each hero is a working diagram of the service rather than a picture of it. It refuses the staffing-site photo collage over a row of icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black ground #0B0C0A, plates one and two steps lighter, brand lime #C9FF4A as whole module fields and the only action colour, white ink, sage-grey secondary ink. One grotesk, Archivo: expanded bold for display, normal width for text. Fine white hairlines draw the grid modules; square corners; the M-wave mark as the line that draws, connects and progresses.

STORY: The visitor sees their own working day mirrored in India, understands five practices run one way, checks the controls (vetting, leads, SOPs, security), compares the cost honestly, and books a call.

FIRST VIEWPORT (home): Left five columns, the headline "Offshore talent. Onshore quality." in expanded caps with the primary Book a call action under it; right seven columns, the live shift dial (two 24-hour rings, U.S. outer and India inner) the visitor drags to set their hours, with zone switches and a live on-shift readout; beneath, a lime module with 48h onboarding and a photograph module, all on visible grid rules.

FORM: Corporate identity standards manual (Swiss/Unimark lineage); grounded candidate 5 of 7; seed: 82d84de5.
Raise from fight-poster: any comparison becomes two halves with a draggable divider (local hire vs Akrostech).
Raise from particle-detector display: heroes isolate one selected element and dim the rest.
Raise from Brodovitch spread: photograph and headline are composed as pairs across a grid seam.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
